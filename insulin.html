Insulin Plant

Costus igneus - Fiery Costus / Spiral Flag

☀️ Part Shade to Full Sun
Description: A vigorous, tropical perennial herb with large, smooth, dark green leaves arranged spirally along the stem. It produces striking, vibrant orange, cone-like flowers. Highly regarded in traditional medicine for its anti-diabetic properties.
Maturity: Flowers consistently during the warm months, primarily from late Summer through Autumn. Leaves can be harvested year-round once the plant is established.
Suggested Pollinators: Primarily pollinated by bees and butterflies, attracted to its brightly colored, nectar-rich orange blooms.
🪴 Moist, Well Drained Soil
Plant Care: Thrives in rich, organic, slightly acidic soil. Requires a warm, humid environment with consistent moisture; do not allow the soil to dry out completely. Best planted in partial shade to protect leaves from scorching. Prune spent flower stems and old stalks down to the base to encourage fresh growth.
📏 Height to 60CM - 1.2M
