Coffee

Coffea arabica - Arabian Coffee

Full Sun to Part Shade
Description: This compact, evergreen shrub features glossy, dark green leaves and clusters of fragrant white flowers that mature into bright red berries. Each cherry typically contains two seeds, which are the coffee beans.
Maturity: Late Autumn to Winter.
Suggested Pollinators: Self-fertile. Does not require a companion plant to set fruit.
Well Drained, Acidic Soil
Tree Care: Plant in a well-drained, rich, organic soil mix with a slightly acidic pH. Choose a sheltered spot protected from harsh afternoon sun and frost. Water regularly to keep the soil consistently moist but never waterlogged.
Height to 2M - 3M
