Nutritional / Superfood Tree

Moringa

Moringa oleifera - Drumstick Tree / Miracle Tree

☀️ Full Sun
Description: A remarkably fast-growing, drought-resistant tropical tree. It carries airy, tripinnate compound leaves, drooping clusters of fragrant creamy-white flowers, and long, triangular seed pods. Every single part of the tree is densely packed with vitamins, proteins, and minerals.
Maturity: Begins producing leaves for harvest within months of planting. Flowering and seed pod development typically set in within its first year of establishment.
Suggested Pollinators: Strongly attracts honeybees, carpenter bees, and sunbirds to its rich, nectar-filled fragrant blooms.
🪴 Sandy, Well Drained, Porous Soil
Tree Care: Excels in warm climates and loves poor, sandy or gravelly well-draining soils. Highly susceptible to root suffocation; water deeply but sparingly, letting the soil dry out entirely between cycles. Prune aggressively back in early spring to control its height and keep nutrient-dense foliage within easy picking reach.
📏 Height to 10M - 12M
