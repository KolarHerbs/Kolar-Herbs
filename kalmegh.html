Medicinal / Herb

Kalmegh

Andrographis paniculata - King of Bitters

☀️ Part Shade to Full Sun
Description: An erect, branchy annual herb featuring slender, dark green stems and lance-shaped leaves. It produces small, delicate white flowers with rose-purple spots. Famously bitter, it is intensely utilized in Ayurveda to boost immunity and support liver and respiratory health.
Maturity: Reaches maturity within 90 to 120 days from planting. Harvesting of leaves and aerial parts is best done just as flowering begins in late Summer or Autumn.
Suggested Pollinators: Heavily relies on small solitary bees and insects attracted to its specialized, hooded blossoms.
🪴 Moist, Loose, Well Drained Soil
Plant Care: Extremely adaptable and grows comfortably across a variety of soils, though it loves fertile, sandy-loam beds. Keep soil evenly moist during the initial stages, but do not let water pool. Thrives beautifully under partial or dappled tree shade, shielding it from severe wilting.
📏 Height to 30CM - 1M
