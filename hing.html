Spice / Medicinal

Hing

Ferula asafoetida - Giant Fennel / Devil's Dung

☀️ Full Sun
Description: A robust, herbaceous perennial featuring hollow stems, fine compound leaves, and large umbels of pale greenish-yellow flowers. The prized spice is derived from the potent, sulfurous oleo-gum-resin extracted from its thick, fleshy taproots.
Maturity: Takes 4 to 5 years of growth before the root system is mature enough to yield high-quality resin. Tapping typically begins in Spring just before flowering.
Suggested Pollinators: Primarily cross-pollinated by wind, bees, and flies attracted to its large, open flowering structures.
🪴 Deep, Well Drained, Sandy Soil
Plant Care: Requires deep, loose, sandy-loam soils to support its extensive taproot system. Extremely drought-tolerant once established; prefers cold, dry winters and hot, arid summers. Highly susceptible to root rot—ensure excellent drainage and water sparingly. Protect young plants from extreme frost.
📏 Height to 1.5M - 2M
