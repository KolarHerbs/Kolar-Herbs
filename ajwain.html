Ajwain

Trachyspermum ammi - Carom / Bishop's Weed

☀️ Full Sun
Description: An annual, herbaceous plant featuring feathery, fern-like leaves and small, delicate umbels of white flowers. The small, oval-shaped seed-like fruits are highly aromatic, offering a pungent taste reminiscent of thyme.
Maturity: Late Winter to Spring (approx. 4–5 months from sowing).
Suggested Pollinators: Self-fertile. The open flower clusters easily attract bees and other beneficial garden insects.
🪴 Well Drained, Loose Soil
Plant Care: Sown directly into alkaline or neutral, well-aerated soil. Prefers moderate watering; avoid soggy conditions as the roots are highly prone to rot. Thrives in warm environments.
📏 Height to 60CM - 90CM
