Spice / Vine

Black Pepper

Piper nigrum - King of Spices

☀️ Part Shade / Dappled Light
Description: A perennial climbing woody vine known for its broad, glossy green leaves and long, hanging spikes of small flowers. The flowers develop into small round berries (drupes) that transition from green to red when fully ripe, which are harvested and dried to create peppercorns.
Maturity: Vines typically begin bearing fruit 3 to 4 years after planting. Harvest periods occur over several months as berry spikes mature.
Suggested Pollinators: Primarily self-fertile, with pollination heavily assisted by rain droplets or high humidity moving pollen down the flower spike.
🪴 Rich, Humus-Rich, Well Drained Soil
Vine Care: Thrives in hot, humid tropical conditions. Requires a sturdy support structure, trellis, or companion tree to climb. Plant in moist, organic-rich soil and maintain consistent watering without waterlogging. Mulch well to retain shallow root moisture and protect from heavy frost or searing afternoon sun.
📏 Height (Length) to 3M - 4M
