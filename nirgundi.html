Medicinal / Aromatic Shrub

Nirgundi

Vitex negundo - Five-Leaved Chaste Tree

☀️ Full Sun
Description: A hardy, aromatic, multi-branched evergreen shrub with distinctive palmate leaves divided into 3 to 5 pointed leaflets. It bears beautiful terminal panicles of small, bluish-purple flowers followed by tiny black drupes. Extensively utilized in herbal medicine for joint pain, inflammation, and as a natural pest deterrent.
Maturity: A very fast grower that can establish within its first year. Flowers intermittently throughout the warm summer and monsoon months. Leaves can be sustainably trimmed all year round.
Suggested Pollinators: Heavily attracts butterflies, honeybees, and smaller pollinators who feast on its prolific purple flower spikes.
🪴 Highly Adaptable, Well Drained Soil
Shrub Care: Extremely easy to maintain and highly resilient. Thrives across a vast range of soils, though it performs best in loose, well-draining loamy mixtures. Highly drought-tolerant once established and resists pests naturally. Prune back in early spring to encourage dense foliage and control its size.
📏 Height to 2M - 4M
