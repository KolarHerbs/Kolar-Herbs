Stevia

Stevia rebaudiana - Sweet Leaf / Candyleaf

☀️ Full Sun to Part Shade
Description: A tender perennial herbal shrub famous for its intensely sweet, lance-shaped green leaves, which are used as a natural calorie-free sugar substitute. Produces small, tubular white flowers in late summer.
Maturity: Late Summer to Autumn (Harvest leaves just before flowering for maximum sweetness).
Suggested Pollinators: Self-fertile, but yields benefit from visits by bees, butterflies, and wind-assisted pollen movement.
🪴 Well Drained, Rich Soil
Herb Care: Plant in a loose, loamy soil rich in organic matter. Keep soil consistently moist but never waterlogged to protect its shallow root system. Feed with a balanced organic fertilizer. Protect from frost and pinch back growing tips regularly to promote a bushy growth habit.
📏 Height to 45CM - 60CM
